Uttarakhand Travel Guide



“Devbhoomi Uttarakhand — where every mountain tells a story and every valley feels like heaven.”

Explore the mountains, temples, wildlife, local culture and delicious food of Uttarakhand on an unforgettable weekend trip.

Uttarakhand, also known as the "Land of Gods", is a beautiful state in northern India. It is famous for its Himalayan mountains, peaceful lakes, temples, forests and adventure activities. It is an excellent destination for a weekend trip or a longe vacation.



1. Top Attractions

Uttarakhand offers attractions for every type of traveller.

2. Culture and Traditions

Uttarakhand has a rich cultural heritage influenced by the Garhwal and Kumaon regions. The state is often called Devbhumi, or the "Land of the Gods", because of its many temples and pilgrimage sites. Traditional folk music, dance, festivals and local handicrafts are an important part of life here.

4. Best Time to Visit

Recommended season: March to June and September to November. These months generally offer pleasant conditions for sightseeing, trekking and exploring hill stations. Winter can be very cold in the higher regions, while the monsoon season may bring heavy rainfall and difficult road conditions in some mountain areas.

For specific destinations, the ideal season can vary. For example, Uttarakhand Tourism recommends March to May and mid-September to December for Nainital.



5. Travel Tips

  • Carry warm clothes because mountain temperatures can change quickly.
  • Wear comfortable shoes if you plan to trek or explore hill areas.
  • Keep drinking water and basic medicines with you while travelling.
  • Check weather and road conditions before travelling to remote areas.
  • Respect local customs, temples and religious places.
  • Keep the mountains clean and avoid single-use plastic wherever possible.

    • Important travel tip: Mountain roads can be affected by weather conditions, so always check official travel and weather updates before beginning a journey to higher-altitude destinations.



    6. Weekend Travel Plan
