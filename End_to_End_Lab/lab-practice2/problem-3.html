Learning Levels & Important Terms

Intermediate

Advanced
Designed for experienced learners who want to study advanced concepts and real-world applications.
Prerequisite

Certification
A certificate provided after successfully completing the course requirements.
Live Session
An interactive online session where students can learn directly from mentors and ask questions.
