ALGEBRIC IDENTITIES


        1. (a+b)2= (a2 + 2b + b2)

Square of a binomial sum — expand by multiplying (a + b) by itself.

        2. (a-b)2= (a2 - 2b + b2)

Square of a binomial difference — expand by multiplying (a - b) by itself.

        3. (a2 + b2)= (a+b)2 - 2ab

Derived from the expansion of (a + b)² — rearrange to isolate the sum of squares.

        4. (a2 - b2)= (a-b) (a+b)

Difference of Squares — always factors as a product of a sum and a difference.

        5. (a+b)3= (a3 + 3a2b +3ab2 + b3)

Cube of a binomial sum — Coefficients follow Pascal's triangle: 1 3 3 1

        6. (a-b)3= (a3 - 3a2b +3ab2 - b3)

Cube of a binomial difference — Odd-powered terms flip sign.

        7. (a3 + b3)= (a+b)(a2 + b2 - ab)

Sum of cubes — Factor two perfect cubes

        8. (a3 - b3)= (ab)(a2 + b2 + ab)

Difference of cubes — Factor difference of two cubes


GEOMETRICAL IDENTITIES


# 2D shapes — area & perimeter


     1. CIRCLE

         Area= πr2

         Circumference= πr

     {here 'r' is radius of a circle, value of π is 22/7= 3.141}


     2. RECTANGLE

         Area= l x b

         Perimeter= 2(l x b)

     {here 'l' and 'b' are the length & breadth of a rectangle respectively.}


     3. SQUARE

         Area= a2

         Perimeter= 4a

     {here 'a' is the side of a square.}


     4. TRIANGLE

        Area= ½(b x h)
     {here 'b' and 'h' are the base and height of a triangle.}

         Perimeter= a+b+c
     {here 'a,b,c' are sides of a triangle.}


     5. TRAPEZIUM

         Area= ½(a + b)*h

     {here 'a,b' are parallel sides and 'h' is height of a trapezium}


     6. RHOMBUS

         Area= ½(d1 x d2)

     {here 'd1,d2' are diagonals of a rhombus}


# Coordinate geometry


     1. DISTANCE FORMULA - Distance between two points

       d= ((x2-x1)2 + (y2-y1)2)½

     {here points (x1,y1) and (x2,y2) are on a plane.}


     2. SECTION FORMULA - Point dividing a segment in ratio m:n

       P(x,y)= [(mx2+nx1)/(m+n) , (my2+ny1)/(m+n)]br>
     {Internal division of a line segment}


MENSTURATION FORMULAS


# 3D shapes — Volume & Surface Area


     1. CUBE

        Surface Area= 6a2

        Volume= a3

    {here 'a' is the edge length.}


     2. CUBOID

        Surface Area= 2(lb+bh+hl)

        Volume= lbh

    {here 'l,b,h' are length, breadth and height of the cuboid respectively.}


     3. CYLINDER

        Total Surface Area= 2πr(r+h)

        Curved Surface Area= 2πrh

        Volume= πr2h

    {here 'r and h' are radius and height of the cylinder respectively.}


     4. CONE

        Total Surface Area= πr(r+l)

        Curved Surface Area= πrl

        Volume= ⅓(πr2h)

    {here 'r, h and l' are radius, height and slant-height(l=(r2+h2)1/2) of the cone respectively.}


     5. SPHERE

        Total Surface Area= 4πr2

        Curved Surface Area= 2πr2

        Volume= 4/3(πr3)

    {here 'r' is radius of a sphere.}


     6. HEMISPHERE

        Total Surface Area= 3πr2

        Curved Surface Area= 2πr2

        Volume= ⅔(πr3)

    {here 'r' is radius of a hemisphere.}
