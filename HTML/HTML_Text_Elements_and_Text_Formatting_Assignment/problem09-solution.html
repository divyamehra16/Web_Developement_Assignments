Student Terms & Rules


# IMPORTANT # : All enrolled students are expected to read and comply with the following terms in their entirety. Ignorance of any rule will not be accepted as an excuse. Violations may result in academic penalties, suspension or expulsion at the discretion of the Academic Committee.

General Rules

MANDATORY : Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode in all classrooms, laboratories, and the library.
Recording of lectures or sessions without explicit written consent from the concerned faculty member is strictly prohibited.

NOTE : Students are encouraged to use the Student Grievance Portal (sgp.glauniv.ac.in) for registering any academic or administrative complaints.
The portal is available 24x7 and responses are guaranteed within five working days.


ATTENDANCE

MANDATORY : A minimum attendance of 75% per subject per semester is compulsory.
Students falling below this threshold will be de-barred from appearing in the End-Semester Examination for the respective subject.

NOTE : Attendance is recorded biometrically at the start of each session. Proxy attendance is a Level-3 disciplinary offence and attracts a minimum penalty of one semester suspension for both the absentee and the proxy.
Medical leave exceeding three consecutive days must be supported by a certificate from a registered medical practitioner countersigned by the college health centre.


EXAMINATION

MANDATORY : Students must report to the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time. No student will be permitted to enter after the first 30 minutes of the examination have elapsed, nor will any student be permitted to leave before 60 minutes have passed.

MANDATORY : Possession of mobile phones, smart watches, Bluetooth earphones, or any electronic device inside the examination hall is strictly prohibited.
Violation will be treated as an unfair means (UFM) case and the student's paper will be immediately cancelled. A UFM case may lead to expulsion from the university.

NOTE : Make-up examinations are granted solely for medical emergencies or unavoidable circumstances approved by the Dean (Academics) in advance. The final decision rests entirely with the Examination Committee.


ASSIGNMENT SUBMISSION

MANDATORY : Plagiarism of any kind is a zero-tolerance offence. All submissions are scanned. A similarity score exceeding 20% from non-self sources will result in a grade of zero for that submission and a formal academic misconduct report being raised.

DEADLINE : Assignments submitted after the posted due date will incur a grade penalty: 10% deduction per day late, up to a maximum of five days. Submissions more than five days late will receive a zero (0/10) and will be marked as "Not Submitted" in the official grade register.
Extension requests must be emailed to the faculty member at least 48 hours before the deadline; extensions are not automatic and require explicit approval.

NOTE : Students are advised to begin assignments well in advance to avoid last-minute technical failures. Technical issues such as internet outages or browser errors are not accepted as valid grounds for late submission after the deadline has passed.


DISCIPLINE

MANDATORY : Students must maintain respectful conduct towards faculty, staff, and fellow students at all times — both on campus and on any official online platform or social media channel associated with the university. Harassment, bullying, or discriminatory behaviour in any form will result in immediate suspension pending an inquiry.

Disciplinary actions are classified into three levels:
   Level 1 (written warning)
   Level 2 (fine and community service)
   Level 3 (suspension or expulsion)
The level assigned depends on the severity and frequency of the offence as determined by the Disciplinary Committee.

NOTE : Students have the right to appeal any disciplinary decision within 15 working days of the written order by submitting a petition to the Vice-Chancellor's Office. The appeal committee's decision is final and binding on all parties.
