Destination Overview

Welcome to Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand is a beautiful state in northern India known for its majestic Himalayan mountains, peaceful valleys, sacred temples, rivers and wildlife. It is popularly known as the "Land of Gods" or Devbhoomi.

From the spiritual atmosphere of Rishikesh and Haridwar to the scenic beauty of Nainital, Mussoorie and Auli, Uttarakhand offers something for every traveller.

Travel Tips

  • Carry warm clothes because mountain temperatures can change quickly.
  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking and trekking.
  • Carry a valid identity card while travelling.
  • Keep drinking water and stay hydrated.
  • Check weather conditions before travelling to high-altitude areas.
  • Respect local culture, temples and environmental guidelines.

Important Travel Notice

Mountain journeys can be affected by weather and road conditions. Always check official travel advisories before visiting remote areas.
