Welcome to FinanceWise

FinanceWise is an educational portal that helps people understand basic banking and personal finance concepts. Learn about savings accounts, cards, loans, interest rates and responsible money management.

"Financial knowledge helps you make better decisions with your money."

Important: Always understand the terms and conditions before choosing any financial product.

Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account allows customers to safely deposit money and earn interest on their balance. It is useful for everyday savings and emergency funds.

Current Account

A current account is mainly designed for businesses and customers who make frequent transactions.

Bank Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows you to make payments using money available in your bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card allows you to borrow money up to an approved limit and repay it later. Responsible use is important because unpaid balances may attract interest and charges.

Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan can be used for various personal expenses. The borrower usually repays the amount through monthly instalments.

Home Loan

A home loan helps eligible customers finance the purchase or construction of a home. The borrowed amount is repaid over an agreed period.

Education Loan

An education loan can help eligible students finance approved education expenses.

Important Notice

Financial products have different eligibility requirements, interest rates, fees and conditions.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save a portion of your income regularly.
  • Maintain an emergency fund.
  • Pay bills and loan EMIs on time.
  • Compare interest rates before borrowing.
  • Read bank charges carefully.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Protect your banking information.

Opening a Bank Account

  1. Choose the type of account.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Provide the required documents.
  5. Complete the verification process.
  6. Make the required initial deposit, if applicable.
  7. Receive your account details.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on borrowed money or earned on deposited money.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid regularly towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or deposited.
Tenure
The total period for which a loan or financial product is maintained.
