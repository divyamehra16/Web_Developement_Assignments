About us

GLA University, Greater Noida is a premier institution of higher education committed to academic excellence and holistic development. Established with the vision of nurturing innovation and intellectual curiosity, the university offers a dynamic learning environment where students are encouraged to think beyond boundaries and contribute meaningfully to society.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, a dedicated faculty of industry experts, and a curriculum designed to meet global standards, GLA University stands as a beacon of quality education in the National Capital Region (NCR).


Programs Offered

GLA University offers a wide spectrum of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across disciplines including Engineering, Management, Law, Pharmacy, and Sciences. Our flagship programs include:

Btech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) ~ A four-year undergraduate program designed to equip students with strong foundations in programming, algorithms, data structures, artificial intelligence, and software development. The curriculum integrates industry projects, internships, and hands-on lab sessions.

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) ~ GLA's regular BCA is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on computer applications, programming, software development and IT. GLA says students can study areas such as C++, Java, Python, web applications, databases, networking, AI and data analytics, with practical labs and projects.

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) ~ GLA Greater Noida currently offers an AI-Enabled BBA for the 2026 admissions batch. It is a 3-year undergraduate program designed to combine traditional business education with practical AI skills.

Master of Business Administration (MBA) ~ A two-year postgraduate program covering marketing, finance, human resources, and entrepreneurship, with regular guest lectures from industry leaders.


Campus Life

Life at GLA University is vibrant and enriching. Beyond academics, students are encouraged to participate in cultural fests, technical hackathons, sports tournaments, and community service drives. Our campus hosts numerous student clubs — from coding clubs and robotics societies to drama groups and photography collectives — ensuring every student finds their passion.

The university's annual fest, Utsav, brings together thousands of students from across the country for days of competition, creativity, and celebration. Residential facilities, a multi-cuisine cafeteria, a well-stocked library, and round-the-clock Wi-Fi ensure students have everything they need to thrive.

Admission

    Important Admission Message:
      GLA University welcomes applications from students across India and abroad.
      We believe in selecting candidates not just on the basis of academic merit, but also on their potential, drive, and creativity.
      Every student who joins us is a future leader in the making.

Admission to undergraduate engineering programs (B.Tech) is based on JEE Main scores or the university's own entrance examination — GLAET (GLA Entrance Test). For management programs, CAT / MAT / CMAT scores are accepted alongside GLAET. Direct admissions may be offered to students with exceptional academic records.

The admission process involves submitting an online application, document verification, and a personal interview for select programs. Scholarships are available for meritorious students and those from economically weaker sections.
