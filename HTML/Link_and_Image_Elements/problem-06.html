INTRODUCTION


Welcome to TechFest 2026, an exciting technology festival where innovation meets creativity. Organized by the College Technology and Cultural Committee.
TechFest brings together students, developers, entrepreneurs, technology enthusiasts, and industry experts under one roof.

From artificial intelligence and robotics to web development, cybersecurity, gaming, and emerging technologies, the festival offers participants an opportunity to learn new skills, demonstrate their talent, compete in challenging events, and connect with like-minded innovators.
