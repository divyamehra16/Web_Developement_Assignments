BREAKING: GLA University Students Win National Hackathon 2026

Team TechVision from B.Tech CSE defeats 500+ teams across India to claim the top prize at HackIndia 2026.


WHAT Happened ?

In a stunning display of innovation and teamwork, Team TechVision from GLA University, Greater Noida claimed the first-place trophy at HackIndia 2026, the country's largest intercollegiate hackathon. The team built LogiLink — a smart logistics platform that connects truck owners, shippers, and warehouse managers in real time — and impressed a panel of judges from India's top technology companies. The victory marks the first time a team from Greater Noida has won the HackIndia Grand Prize in the competition's eight-year history.



WHERE it took place ?

The finale was held at the Bharat Mandapam Convention Centre, New Delhi — one of India's largest and most prestigious event venues. Over 2,000 participants from 38 states and union territories gathered across the two-day event on 23 and 24 August 2026. Teams competed in six tracks: HealthTech, FinTech, EdTech, AgriTech, GreenTech, and Logistics and Supply Chain — the track in which Team TechVision competed and excelled.



WHEN and HOW they won ?

The competition began at 9:00 AM on 23 August and teams had exactly 36 hours to design, build, and present a working prototype. Team TechVision submitted their project — a fully functional web application with real-time truck tracking, automated load matching, and a driver rating system — with four hours to spare. During the final presentation, the judges praised the team for the depth of market research, the clean user interface, and the scalability of the backend architecture. "This is not a student project — this is a startup," said one judge from a leading venture capital firm.



WHY it Matters ?

India's logistics sector is worth over ₹14 lakh crore and is plagued by inefficiencies — empty trucks returning from deliveries, lack of real-time freight pricing, and poor communication between drivers and shippers. LogiLink directly addresses these pain points. The team's solution could save the industry thousands of crores annually if deployed at scale. Beyond the prize money of ₹5 lakh, the team has been offered mentorship by three venture capital firms and an invitation to present at the upcoming India Tech Summit 2026 in Bengaluru. For GLA University, this is not just a trophy — it is proof that students from Tier-2 cities can compete and win on the national stage.
