GLA UNIVERSITY, Greater Noida, UttarPradesh


About the College

GLA University Greater Noida is an off-campus branch of GLA University Mathura, located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh.
It is approved by AICTE and UGC, and holds a NAAC A+ accreditation. The campus is located at 15A, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh.
It is an industry-integrated campus. And is also ranked #1 in Uttar Pradesh by Dainik Jagran.GLA University has a 51,000+ global alumni network, with 6,000+ professionals working abroad.

Courses Offered

GLA offers undergraduate and postgraduate and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines. Some of the major programs include:

B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) along with specialisation
B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering
B.Tech in Civil Engineering
B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering
BBA (Bachelors of Business Administration)
BCA (Bachelors of Computer Applications)

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA is a blend of tight academic schedules,industry-focused training and full of opportunities beyond the classroom.
Though the campus is not spread across a vast area and yet it is equipped with modern facilities including well-stocked libraries, advanced computer labs, sports complexes and well-furnished classrooms.
The university continuously updates its curriculum to align with the latest industry trends and technological advancements, ensuring that students are job-ready upon graduation.
