SCIENCE NOTES



CHEMISTRY


Chemistry is the branch of science that studies matter, its properties, composition, structure, and the changes it undergoes.

Common Chemical Formula used in chemistry;

     1. WATER  -  H2O

     2. OXYGEN GAS  -  O2

     3. CARBONDIOXIDE GAS  -  CO2

     4. SULPHURIC ACID  -  H2SO4

     5. GLUCOSE  -  C6H12O6

PHYSICS


Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, forces, and their interactions.

Basic formulas used in physic;

     1. SPEED  -  Distance x Time

     2. VELOCITY  -  Displacement x Time

     3. ACCELERATION  -  (v-u)/t  {here 'u,v,t' are initial velocity, final velocity and time respectively.}

     4. FORCE  -  ma  {here 'm,a' are mass and acceleration of a particle respectively.}

     5. KINETIC ENERGY  -  ½(mv2)  {here 'm,v' are mass and velocity of a particle respectively.}

     6. PRESSURE  -  F/A  {here 'F,A' are Force and Area of a particle/body respectively. Area is commonly measured in m2.}

BIOLOGY


Biology is the branch of science that studies living organisms, including their structure, functions, growth, and reproduction.

Basic formulas/expression used in Biology.

     1. Photosynthesis  -  6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

     2. Aerobic-Respiration  -   C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + Energy

     3. BMI(Body Mass Index)  -  m/h2(in m)  {here 'm,h' are mass and height of a body respectively.}

Quick Revision


> H2O = Water

> CO2 = Carbon dioxide

> O2 = Oxygen

> m2 = Square metre

> F = ma = Force

> Photosynthesis = 6CO2 + 6H2O
