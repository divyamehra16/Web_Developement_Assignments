About the University

GLA University was established in 1998 under the GLA Institute of Technical Research and Education Society and was granted university status by the University Grants Commission (UGC) in 2010. Located on National Highway 2, the Mathura-Delhi Road in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, the university spans a lush, fully residential campus of over 125 acres and currently serves more than 18,000 students from across India.

The university holds an A+ grade accreditation from NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council) and is consistently ranked among the top private universities in North India by the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF). GLA has signed MoUs with over 200 industry partners, including multinational tech companies, national research institutes, and international universities across the United Kingdom, Germany, and Canada.

At its core, GLA University is committed to three foundational values: academic excellence, industry readiness, and holistic personal development. Research and innovation are embedded into every undergraduate and postgraduate program, ensuring that students graduate not merely as degree-holders but as critical thinkers and problem-solvers prepared for a rapidly changing world.



Departments

GLA University operates through ten academic schools, each housing one or more specialised departments. The institute follows a credit-based semester system aligned with the guidelines of the AICTE and the UGC.


School of Engineering & Technology

The School of Engineering & Technology is the largest department in the university, offering programs in Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering. The school houses fourteen state-of-the-art laboratories and is home to the university's flagship Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (CAIDS).

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering alone enrolls approximately maximum students per year across its B.Tech programs.
Faculty strength stands at 96 permanent members, of whom 72 hold doctoral degrees from premier institutions including the IITs, NITs, and foreign universities.


School of Mangemenr

The School of Management offers the flagship BBA BCA program with specialisations in Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Business Analytics, and International Business. The school's placement cell has consistently achieved a 97% placement rate over the past five consecutive years, with the highest package recorded at ₹42 LPA in 2024.

B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering

The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science & Engineering is a four-year, eight-semester program. The curriculum is divided into three phases:
Foundation Phase; covering mathematics, physics, and programming fundamentals in the first year.
Core Engineering Phase; covering data structures, operating systems, databases, computer networks, and software engineering in years two and three.
Specialisation and Industry Phase in the final year, during which students select an elective track and complete a six-month industry internship.

Elective tracks currently offered include: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Cloud Computing & DevOps, Full Stack Web Development, and Data Science & Analytics. Each track is co-designed with industry partners and updated every academic year to reflect current industry requirements.

Grading System

The university follows a 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system. The minimum passing grade for any subject is D, which corresponds to a grade point of 4.0 and a percentage score of 40% or above. Students who score below 40% in any subject must appear for the supplementary examination conducted within six weeks of the main result declaration

A student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 6.0 at the end of each academic year to be promoted to the next year. Students with a CGPA below 6.0 at year-end are placed on academic probation and must improve their scores before the commencement of the following semester.



Campus Facilities

GLA University's residential campus is designed to be a complete, self-sufficient academic ecosystem. Students have access to world-class infrastructure that supports both rigorous academic pursuit and healthy personal development throughout the year

Central Library

The library operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week throughout the academic year.

Students may borrow up to four books simultaneously for a period of fourteen days. A fine of ₹5 per day per book is charged for overdue returns.

Important Information

The following notices apply to all currently enrolled students for the academic year 2026-27 Students are advised to read each item carefully and take note of all applicable deadlines.

Fee Payment

Tuition and hostel fees for the Even Semester (January-June 2027) must be paid in full by 15 December 2026. Payments made after this date will attract a late fee of ₹500 per week. Students who have not cleared their dues by 31 December 2025 will have their LMS and library access suspended until the outstanding balance is settled.

Scholarship and Financial Aid

GLA University administers the Merit Scholarship Programme for students who score 90% or above in their Class XII Board Examinations and maintain a CGPA of 8.5 or above throughout their program. The scholarship covers 50% of the annual tuition fee and is renewed each year subject to academic performance

Need-based financial aid is also available through the university's Student Welfare Fund. Eligible students — those whose family annual income is below ₹5,00,000 — may apply year-round by submitting Form SWF-03, along with a certified income affidavit, to the Office of Student Welfare on the basement of the Administrative Block.

Internship and Placemnents

The Training & Placement Cell (TPC) will begin registering final-year B.Tech students for the campus placement drive of 2026-27 from 1 August 2026. Eligible students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.5, no active backlogs, and must have submitted their updated resume on the TPC portal by 20 August 2026. Students who miss this window will not be included in the first phase of the placement drive.
