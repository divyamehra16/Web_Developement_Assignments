INTRODUCTION

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering at GLA University, Greater Noida, offers a rigorous four-year B.Tech programme accredited by NBA. Our curriculum blends theoretical foundations with hands-on laboratory practice — spanning algorithms, systems programming, machine learning, and software engineering. This portal is the single authoritative source for semester timetables, subject syllabi, faculty contacts, and academic regulations for Sem I students.

Academic Information

Lecture :
A classroom session where theoretical concepts are explained by the faculty.

Lab :
A practical session where students perform experiments using computers or laboratory equipment.

Tutorial :
A guided academic session focused on problem-solving and additional practice.

Internal Assessment :
Academic evaluation conducted during the semester to measure student progress.

Practical :
A hands-on activity in which students apply theoretical knowledge to practical problems.

Academic Rules

  • Attendance is mandatory for all lectures and practical classes.
  • Students must carry their ID cards while attending department activities.
  • Students must submit assignments before the announced deadline.
  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during lectures.
  • Students must maintain discipline and respect faculty members.
  • Laboratory equipment must be handled carefully.

Important Academic Notice: Students must check the department notice board regularly for changes in the timetable, examinations, assignments, and practical schedules.
