Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day student celebration at GLA University Greater Noida Campus. The event is designed to encourage creativity, innovation, leadership, teamwork, and healthy competition among students.

The fest will include technical events, cultural performances, creative competitions, gaming activities, and student-led events.

GLA Greater Noida promotes a vibrant campus life through student events, clubs, cultural activities, sports, hackathons, and other opportunities for student development.

Registration Process

Registration Deadline: 5 April 2026, 11:59 PM

The deadline above is sample data for this HTML assignment. Students should follow the official announcement for the actual fest registration deadline.

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Select the competition or event in which you want to participate.
  3. Enter your name, student ID, course, year, and contact details.
  4. Select individual or team participation where applicable.
  5. Pay the registration fee if the event requires one.
  6. Submit the registration form before the deadline.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.
  8. Report to the assigned venue before the event begins.

Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid GLA University student ID card.
  • Participants must report to the venue at least 30 minutes before their scheduled event.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • Students must maintain discipline and respect other participants.
  • University property and equipment must be used responsibly.
  • Participants must follow event-specific rules.
  • The decision of the judging panel will be final for competitions.

Fest Terms and Definitions

Open Event
An event open to all students who satisfy the eligibility conditions.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more students participate together.
Individual Event
A competition in which a student participates alone.
Registration
The process through which a student officially enrolls for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for planning and managing a particular fest event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where shortlisted participants compete for the winning positions.
