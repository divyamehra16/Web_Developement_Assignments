GLA University, Greater Noida offers undergraduate and postgraduate across Engineering, Management and the Sciences. Admissions are conducted on a merit-cum-entrance basis, with central counselling sessions held every semester.


Eligibility Criteria

  • Undergraduate Programmes (B.Tech/ BBA/ BCA)

    • Candidates must have passed Class XII (10+2) from a recognised board with a minimum aggregate of 50% in the relevant stream. Mathematics is compulsory for B.Tech admissions.

  • Postgraduate Programmes (MBA/ MCA)

    • A recognised Bachelor's degree in the relevant discipline with a minimum of 55% aggregate marks is required. Candidates appearing for their final semester examination may apply provisionally.

    Application Process

    Follow each step in the exact order shown below. Skipping a step may invalidate your application.
    1. Visit the official admission portal and register with a valid Email address and mobile number to receive your unique Application ID.

    2. Fill out the online application form, selecting your preferred programme and specialisation. Save the form as a draft before final submission.

    3. Upload all the documents in the specified format (PDF / JPEG, max 2 MB each) using teh document upload section in the portal.

    4. Pay the Non-Refundable application fee online via Net Banking, UPI, or Debit/Credit Card. Download and retain the payment receipt.

    5. Appear for the University Entrance Test (GLAT) on the date communicated to your registered email.

    6. Check the published Merit List on the portal and report for in-person counselling on the scheduled date with all original documents.

    7. Complete Seat Allotment and Fee Payment within 48 hours of counselling to confirm your admission. Seats lapse after the deadline.

    8. Collect your Admission Confirmation Letter from the Registrar's Office and report to your department on the specified orientation date.

    Required Documents

    Prepare the following documents before beginning your application. Order of submission does not matter, but all items are mandatory.

    • Class X (secondary) marksheet & certificate [photocopy]

    • Class XII (senior secondary) marksheet & certificate [photocopy]

    • Transfer Certificate [original]

    • Migration Certificate {if from another state board}

    • Character Certificate [original]

    • Aadhar Card [original + photocopy]

    • Passport-size photographs [2 copies minimum]

    • Category Certificate [original + photocopy]

    • Gap Year Affidavit {if education gap exists} [original]

    • Medical certificate

    • Undertaking Form (form available in the college portal)

    • Physically Handicapped Certificate (if applicable)

    Important Terms

    • Eligibility

      The minimum academic qualifications and criteria that an applicant must meet to be considered for admission to a particular programme at the university.


    • Merit list

      A ranked list of applicants prepared by the university based on their performance in the entrance exam and qualifying examination marks, used to allocate seats.


    • Counselling

      A scheduled session where merit-listed candidates report in person to verify original documents, exercise their programme preferences, and confirm their seat allotment.


    • Scholarship

      A financial award granted to eligible students based on academic excellence, sports achievement, or economic need that partially or fully offsets tuition fees.


    • Registration

      The formal process of creating an account on the admissions portal and obtaining an Application ID, which is the first step in the official application workflow.
