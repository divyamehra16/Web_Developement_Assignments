Desi Delight

Welcome to Desi Delight, a family-friendly restaurant serving delicious Indian cuisine made with fresh ingredients, traditional spices, and modern presentation.

Our Story

Desi Delight was born from a single obsession: to cook Indian food with the patience it deserves. Our recipes travel centuries — from Mughal court kitchens to coastal fishing villages — and land at your table with every shortcut ruthlessly removed. We source saffron from Pampore, mustard oil from Rajasthan, and our spice blends are ground fresh each morning. Every dish on this menu has a reason to exist. We hope you taste it.
