Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a large-scale career and education event designed to connect students, graduates, job seekers, educational institutions and employers under one platform.

The event provides visitors with opportunities to explore career options, internships, placements, scholarships, apprenticeships and higher education programs.

Attendees can interact with representatives from leading organizations, participate in expert sessions, receive career guidance and learn about current opportunities.

Event Details

  • Date: 15 October 2026
  • Time: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Venue: India Expo Centre, Greater Noida
  • Entry: Free with prior registration
  • Audience: Students, graduates and job seekers

Objectives of the Fair

  1. Connect students with employers and educational institutions.
  2. Provide information about career opportunities.
  3. Promote internships, placements and apprenticeships.
  4. Help students understand different career sectors.
  5. Provide guidance for higher education and scholarships.
"Our goal is to create a platform where every participant can discover opportunities, meet the right people and take the next step toward a successful career."
— Event Organizing Committee

Preparation Guide

Preparing before attending the fair can help participants make better use of their time and interact confidently with organizations.

Important Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research organizations before visiting their booths.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Carry copies of your resume if required.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and representatives.
  • Keep your registration confirmation available.
  • Arrive early to attend your preferred sessions.

Notice: Session seating may be limited. Participants are encouraged to arrive before the scheduled session time.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue and check in.
  4. Collect your event information.
  5. Visit participating organizations.
  6. Attend selected workshops and sessions.
  7. Interact with recruiters and counselors.
  8. Follow up on relevant opportunities after the event.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is registration required?

Yes. Participants should register before attending the event.

Q: Can school and college students attend?

Yes. Students, graduates and job seekers can participate, subject to individual organization eligibility requirements.

Q: Should I bring my resume?

Yes. Participants seeking internships or jobs are encouraged to bring an updated resume.

Q: Are the sessions free?

Yes. The listed sessions are included with event registration.
