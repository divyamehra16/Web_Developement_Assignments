About HealFirst Clinic

HealFirst Clinic is a patient-focused healthcare facility committed to providing quality, compassionate, and accessible medical care. The clinic aims to create a comfortable and welcoming environment where patients can receive professional guidance, timely treatment, and personalized attention.

With a focus on patient well-being, preventive care, accurate diagnosis, and effective treatment. HealFirst Clinic strives to build long-term trust with individuals and families. Our mission is simple: put patients first and make healthcare better, safer, and more caring.

Doctor & Specialties

  1. General Physician

    2. A medical doctor who provides primary and comprehensive healthcare for people of all ages. They diagnose and treat common illnesses, manage ongoing health conditions, provide preventive care, and guide patients toward healthier lifestyles. A general physician is often the first healthcare professional a patient consults when they experience a new health concern.

    Dr. Aarav Mehta - General Physician : Compassionate, comprehensive healthcare for you and your family. From routine check-ups and common illnesses to chronic condition management and preventive care, our general physician provides personalized guidance focused on your overall well-being.

  2. Cardiologist

    3. A Cardiologist is a medical specialist who focuses on the heart and blood vessels. They diagnose, treat, and help prevent heart-related conditions while providing personalized care to support long-term cardiovascular health.

    Dr. Isha Sharma - Cardiologist : Expert care for a healthier heart. From heart check-ups and risk assessment to the diagnosis and management of cardiovascular conditions, our cardiology services focus on early detection, personalized treatment, and better heart health.

  3. Dermatologist

    4. A Dermatologist is a medical specialist who focuses on the health of the skin, hair, and nails. They diagnose and treat a wide range of skin conditions and provide guidance for maintaining healthy skin.

    Dr. Rohan Verma - Dermatologist : Expert care for healthy skin, hair, and nails. From common skin concerns and allergies to acne, infections, and other dermatological conditions, our dermatologist provides personalized care focused on healthier, confident skin.

  4. Pediatrician

    5. A Pediatrician is a medical specialist who provides healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents. They monitor growth and development, diagnose and treat childhood illnesses, and provide guidance on nutrition, vaccinations, and overall well-being.

    Dr. Ananya Gupta - Pediatrician: Gentle, specialized care for growing children. From routine check-ups and vaccinations to common childhood illnesses and developmental guidance, our pediatrician provides compassionate care focused on your child's health and well-being.

  5. Orthopedic Specialist

    6. An Orthopedic Specialist is a medical doctor who focuses on the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and other parts of the musculoskeletal system. They diagnose and manage injuries, joint problems, and conditions affecting movement and mobility.

    Dr. Vikram Singh - Orthopedic Specialist: Expert care for stronger bones and better mobility. From joint pain and sports injuries to bone, muscle, and movement-related conditions, our orthopedic specialist provides personalized care to help you stay active and move with confidence.

Appointment Preferences

  1. Fill in the patient intake form.
  2. Select your preferred specialty and doctor.
  3. Choose an available appointment date and time.
  4. Provide your symptoms or reason for the visit.
  5. Submit the appointment request.
  6. Wait for confirmation from the clinic.
