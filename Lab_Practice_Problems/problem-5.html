Technology

Artificial Intelligence Is Rewriting the College Curriculum

Artificial intelligence is no longer a subject reserved for postgraduate research labs. Universities across India and the world are weaving AI modules into undergraduate engineering, management, and even arts programmes. Students who graduate without at least a foundational understanding of machine learning risk entering a workforce that has already moved on.

Leading institutions such as IIT Bombay and BITS Pilani have launched dedicated AI&ML elective tracks. Faculty report that student enrolment in these electives has doubled over the last two academic years, reflecting genuine demand rather than administrative nudging.

Education

How to Actually Study Smarter, Not Harder

Every student has heard the phrase study smarter, not harder, but few have been shown what that looks like in practice. The science of learning — cognitive load theory, spaced repetition, and active recall — gives concrete answers that replace vague motivational advice.

Spaced repetition means revisiting material at increasing intervals rather than cramming it all the night before an exam. Research consistently shows this method improves long-term retention by up to 200 % compared with massed practice. Tools such as Anki make it effortless to implement.

Active recall goes a step further: instead of re-reading your notes, you close them and test yourself. The struggle to retrieve information strengthens the neural pathway that stores it — a phenomenon researchers call the testing effect.

Online Certifications That Actually Matter to Recruiters

Not every certificate carries the same weight on a resume. Recruiters at top firms have been candid: they look for certifications from recognised bodies — Google, AWS, Microsoft, NASSCOM — over generic online badges that require no proctoring and no rigour.

The Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate and the AWS Cloud Practitioner are currently the two most frequently cited credentials in Indian tech hiring portals. Both can be completed within three to six months alongside regular coursework.

The key differentiator is not how many certificates you collect, but whether you can demonstrate applied projects alongside each one. A certificate without a project is a claim; a project with a certificate is proof.



Career

Your Career Starts on Day One: A Practical Roadmap

Placement season in the final year catches too many students unprepared. The students who walk away with the best offers are rarely the smartest in the room — they are the ones who started building their profile on Day One of their undergraduate journey.

The roadmap is simpler than it looks. Year One: master the fundamentals of your discipline and set up a LinkedIn profile and a GitHub account. Year Two: complete at least one internship, however short. Year Three: build and ship two personal projects that solve real problems. Year Four: refine, apply, and interview with genuine confidence rather than desperation.



Campus Life

Managing Mental Health in the Middle of Semester Chaos

Academic pressure, hostel friction, financial stress, and the constant scroll of social media create a perfect storm for student mental health. Acknowledging this reality is not weakness — it is the first step toward building the resilience that will serve you long after college ends.

Simple, consistent habits matter more than dramatic lifestyle overhauls. A fixed sleep schedule, 30 minutes of physical activity per day, and three meals eaten without a screen in sight form a baseline that psychologists consistently recommend. Your college counsellor is a resource, not a last resort — use them early and often.

Remember: the students who look like they have everything figured out rarely do. The campus experience is supposed to be messy, uncertain, and full of false starts. That is not a bug; it is the entire point.
